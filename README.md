# The Sanctuary Spine - D417

Interactive adult ICU room concept by **Mustafa Hisham Ali, Khalifa University**, prepared for the SSMC ICU Interior Design Student Competition.

## Open the interactive room

https://mrmha.github.io/d417-sanctuary-spine/

The single `index.html` contains the room, six camera views, lighting modes, clinical scenario illustrations, technical concept drawings and a simulated patient communication / AI-safety interface. No installation or account is required to view the published page. You can also download `index.html` and open it locally.

## Status

This is a student design concept and local interactive simulation. Equipment geometry, clearances, structural mounting, services, infection prevention and AI integrations require specialist validation. Displayed clinical data is synthetic. This is not a medical product or clinical decision system.

Routine modeled foot clearance is approximately 1205 mm; it reduces to 951 mm with bed extension, 905 mm in the modeled airway position, and 651 mm with airway positioning plus extension. Casework/cart access also narrows when the drawer is open. Do not interpret a successful software check as clinical approval.

The presentation renders are illustrative AI-enhanced images; the model drawings are the proposed geometry reference. AI assisted design development, research synthesis, coding, review and visual presentation. The original project revision is v6, dated 17 November 2026. The public copy omits the entrant's student identifier while preserving the interactive design.
