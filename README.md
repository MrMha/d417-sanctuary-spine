# The Sanctuary Spine - D417 / Gold Master
Mustafa Hisham Ali, Khalifa University. SSMC ICU design student competition.

Open https://mrmha.github.io/d417-sanctuary-spine/ or download index.html and open locally.

The standalone room includes nine vector plates, five clinical scenarios, staff-first privacy, a 24-hour target schedule and bounded multilingual AAC. Typed communication and embedded assets work offline. Live microphone recognition is optional, initiated by the viewer, and may use the browser vendor's online service after explicit consent. No camera is acquired.

All clinical data are synthetic. Lighting, acoustics, 350 kg hoist capacity, glazing response, outlet quantities and 72-hour retrofit are engineering targets or planning allowances, not measurements or approval. The adopted 8520 x 4465 mm design clear-face shell and proposed northeast partition need survey confirmation. The source pier is retained at X4890..5990 mm: the 5440 source dimension is to its centreline. The supplied 1170 mm angled-wall claim conflicts with its supplied endpoints (approximately 2317 mm); the proposed endpoints are used explicitly.

Gold Master adds a 250 mm staff-planned west bed offset (454 mm nominal east gap / 950 mm west band, versus 204 mm centered), a clean BCMA mini-panel, separate wet-side sealed-liner containment and a perimeter HEPA supply / low-return design intent. The requested return zone overlaps wet fixtures; the unobstructed intake is at X8100..8460. Pressure balance, airflow distribution and clinical access proceed through Stage-2 mock-up / MEP commissioning. Bed offsets are design scenarios, never commands to move a connected patient.

Conventional life-support, alarms and hardwired nurse call remain independent. AI does not actuate treatment, clinical devices, beds, doors or HVAC.

Final polish adds the proposed staff-initiated two-way reassurance link as a clearly labelled local screen preview, prayer-mat drawer, warm reading light, USB-C sockets and a recessed northern family privacy track outside hoist travel. Live media are not connected. Native physically based rendering uses ACES, 4096 PCF soft shadows, calibrated cove fill, a local room reflection probe and support contact occlusion. All nine SVG plates remain byte-identical to the preceding Gold Master.
